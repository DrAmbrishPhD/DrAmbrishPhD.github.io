01 / ABOUT

Evidence with a
purpose.

AMBRISH SINGH · PHD

I am an epidemiologist and health economist with more than a decade of experience across health economics and outcomes research, real-world evidence, evidence synthesis and health technology assessment.

My doctoral research at the Menzies Institute for Medical Research, University of Tasmania, examined the epidemiology and health economics of early knee osteoarthritis.

Today, my work brings together clinical evidence, population health, comparative effectiveness and economic thinking to answer questions that matter to patients, clinicians and healthcare decision-makers.
