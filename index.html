01 / ABOUT

A research profile
built around
evidence.

I am an epidemiologist and health economist with more than a decade of experience in health economics and outcomes research, evidence synthesis, real-world evidence and health technology assessment.

My work sits deliberately between primary and secondary research. I have worked with observational and routine healthcare data, developed evidence for clinical and economic questions, and led systematic and targeted reviews, meta-analyses and comparative evidence programmes.

My PhD at the Menzies Institute for Medical Research, University of Tasmania, focused on the epidemiology and health economics of early knee osteoarthritis. That foundation continues to shape how I approach evidence: understand the population, interrogate the data, synthesise what is known and identify what decision-makers still need to know.
