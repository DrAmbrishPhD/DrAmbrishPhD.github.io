01 / ABOUT

Evidence with a
purpose.

I am an epidemiologist and health economist with more than a decade of experience across health economics and outcomes research, real-world evidence, evidence synthesis and health technology assessment.

My academic training includes a PhD from the Menzies Institute for Medical Research, University of Tasmania, where my research focused on the epidemiology and health economics of early knee osteoarthritis.

My work spans systematic and targeted literature reviews, meta-analysis, network meta-analysis, burden of disease, outcomes research and evidence generation for healthcare decision-making.
