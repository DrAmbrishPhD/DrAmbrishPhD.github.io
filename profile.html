About Ambrish Singh

Dr. Ambrish Singh holds a PhD in Epidemiology and Health Economics from the University of Tasmania. His work spans primary and secondary research, including longitudinal epidemiology, observational and healthcare database studies, burden-of-disease research, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, network meta-analysis, economic evaluation and HTA/value evidence.

His research and professional work connects population health questions with evidence synthesis and healthcare decision-making, with interests spanning cardiometabolic health, obesity, diabetes, osteoarthritis and musculoskeletal health, real-world evidence, health economics and evidence-based decision making.
